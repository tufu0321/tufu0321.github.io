## About me

I am a *Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter* (research staff member) at the **Chair of Hydromechanics and Hydrosystem Modelling (LH2)**, **Institute for Modelling Hydraulic and Environmental Systems (IWS)**, University of Stuttgart, Germany.

My research connects continuum modelling, mathematical analysis and scientific computing. I am particularly interested in how pore-scale phenomena—including interfacial motion, evaporation and transport—can be described accurately at larger scales.
