<div class="page-lede">I study the mathematical structure and numerical simulation of flow, phase changes and transport processes in heterogeneous porous materials, with an emphasis on bridging scales.</div>

## Phase-field and moving interfaces

<div class="tg-feature"><h3>Diffuse-interface models for multiphase systems</h3><p>Diffuse-interface formulations represent moving phase boundaries without explicit interface tracking. My work considers evaporation in porous media and related interfacial processes involving multiphase transport and mineral precipitation.</p></div>

## Multiscale upscaling

<div class="tg-feature"><h3>From pore-scale physics to effective equations</h3><p>Microscopic geometries strongly influence macroscopic transport. I investigate homogenization and upscaling approaches for coupled flow, heat transfer and species transport, including local thermal non-equilibrium models.</p></div>

## Numerical methods

<div class="tg-feature"><h3>Reliable computation of coupled PDEs</h3><p>I work with numerical techniques for complex transport systems, especially finite-volume methods. Key interests include physical consistency, robust solution algorithms, verification and reproducible scientific simulation.</p></div>
